# Security Policy

This policy applies to NanoWarp. Its maintainers and reporting channels are separate from those of upstream Warp.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Do not publish exploit details, credentials, private logs, or a vulnerability reproduction in a public issue or pull request.

NanoWarp has not yet published a private security contact, and GitHub private vulnerability reporting is not currently enabled for this repository. To arrange private disclosure, [request a private contact from a maintainer](https://github.com/Rycen7822/NanoWarp/issues/new) without including vulnerability details or personal information. Wait for a private channel before sharing the report.

Once a private channel is available, include the affected version and OS, impact, minimal reproduction, and relevant diagnostics with secrets removed. Do not assume that upstream Warp's email addresses or advisory form reach NanoWarp maintainers.
